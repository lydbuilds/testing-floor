PROTOCOL 05 / BEHAVIORAL OUTPUT

Innie Action Forecast

Hang in there.

Time Needed1–5 min

Use when

You cannot determine your Innie’s emotion, but you can identify what action you are about to take.

Ask yourself

“What is my Innie about to do?”

“Will that help or hurt the situation?”

Select the predicted action or actions
What is the likely impact?

Your projected behavior has not yet been authorized.

Practice calibration: overheard criticism

You overhear criticism of your performance during a high-stress, high-stakes situation where you were forced to deliver or accept a disappointing outcome. The criticism comes from someone with very little information about the circumstances or what you achieved within them.

You may complete this activity privately. Sharing your Innie First Draft is optional.
