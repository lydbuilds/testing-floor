PROTOCOL 02 / SYSTEMS REPORTING

Innie Systems Reporting Check

The Board will not be contributing to this meeting verbally.

Time Needed1–5 min

Use when

You cannot identify the emotion, but your body or behavior is clearly generating system alerts.

Step 1

“What signals are my Innie systems reporting?”

Step 2

Scan each area without trying to interpret it yet.

Step 3

“What does this report suggest my Outie should do next?”

Jaw
Shoulders
Breathing
Chest
Stomach
Hands
Attention
Behavior
Which next actions are available?

You do not need to know the emotion. The system report is sufficient.

Practice calibration: agenda expansion

You are preparing to facilitate a high-stakes meeting. Five minutes before it begins, leadership adds two agenda items and asks you to “keep the meeting moving.” Complete a systems scan before replying.
