ORIENTATION / THE YOU YOU ARE

The Innie reports. The Outie responds.

01

Your Innie

Your internal operating state: first reactions, body signals, activation, confusion, urgency, capacity, unmet needs, and what you privately want to say.

02

Your Outie

Your external response and professional action: the words you communicate, questions you ask, boundaries you set, actions you choose, and decisions you make.

03

Innie First Draft

The unfiltered internal reaction. It is information, not an instruction.

04

Outie Final Draft

The clear, professional translation of the internal signal.

The goal is not to silence your Innie. The goal is to understand the signal so your Outie can respond intentionally. No belly buttons will be assessed.

PROTOCOL 01 / WELLNESS CHECKPOINT

Decision Readiness Index

Please try to enjoy each feeling equally.

Time Needed1–3 min

Use when

You cannot name the feeling, but you can identify how activated your Innie feels.

Ask yourself

“What activation level is my Innie reporting, and what action is appropriate for my Outie?”

The goal is not to reach Level 1. The goal is to choose an action appropriate to the level you are in.

Select your current activation level

Select a level to receive an approved grounding action.

Practice calibration: “Alignment Meeting”

You have been invited to a meeting titled “Alignment Meeting.” There is no agenda. Six people have accepted. The organizer says, “We’ll figure it out once everyone is in the room, but you can go ahead and kick us off.”

  1. Identify your Innie’s activation level.
  2. Use the DRI to determine your Outie’s next action.
  3. Ask: “What would help my Innie move down one level?”
