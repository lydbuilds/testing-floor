THE WELLNESS CENTER / PATIENT PROTOCOLS

Which protocol should I use?

Locate the closest observable condition. An emotion word is not required.

If you notice…Use…
“I do not know how activated I am.”Decision Readiness Index
“I do not know the emotion, but my body is reacting.”Innie Systems Reporting Check
“I know I need something, but not what.”Innie Wellness Request Form
“I need to respond without overcommitting.”Testing Floor Communication Protocol
“I am about to do something reactive.”Innie Action Forecast
Patient responses may remain private. You are not required to disclose an emotion, personal experience, diagnosis, Innie First Draft, system report, or selected answer.
